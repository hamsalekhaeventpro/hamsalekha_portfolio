HOW WE WORK

From first call to final strike.

Five stages, one team and no surprises on the day.

01

Brief

Tell us the date, venue, guest count and the feeling you want to create. Share your planner's or decorator's moodboard if you have one. We listen first.

02

Recce & Plan

We visit the venue, measure the space, check power and access, and draw the layout: lighting, genset placement, truss, stage and seating.

03

Proposal

You receive a clear, itemised proposal listing every piece, quantity and timeline. Choose from the catalogue or let us curate, and revise until it is right.

04

Build

Our crew loads in to schedule, raises structures, lights the space, places furniture and tests power. Your site lead walks the venue with you before handover.

05

Show & Strike

Technicians and crew stay through the event. When the last guest leaves, we strike cleanly and return the venue the way we found it.
